Research

Beyond building apps, I’ve done research and presented it at an international conference.

Presented at AIRIM 2026, November 5 to 7, SRM University-AP

Staged Hyperparameter Search for a Feature-Selective edRVFL Ensemble in Cross-Subject Drowsy-Driving Detection from Brain Signals

By Kalyani Murakonda, Shaik Akbar Bashee, Ajaykumar Sanaka and Prabhujit Mohapatra

A drowsiness detector is only useful if it works on drivers it has never seen. We tuned an ensemble of deep random vector functional-link layers on EEG features with a two-stage, coarse-to-fine search, using only 65 candidates and validating on the training drivers alone.

Tested by leaving out one driver at a time on 11 drivers, it beat SVM, random forest, EEGNet, ShallowCNN, the subject-matching model and ICNN, and earned the best average ranking of the eight methods compared.
